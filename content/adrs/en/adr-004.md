**Context.** In SSG the theme flashes on first load if decided client-side after paint.

**Decision.** After prototyping two options (module vs. a custom VueUse script), I chose `@nuxtjs/color-mode`: it injects its anti-flash script before first paint. Storage with the brand prefix `dannrodd-theme`.

**Consequences.** Zero theme flash, one dependency that solves the whole case instead of maintaining a custom script.
