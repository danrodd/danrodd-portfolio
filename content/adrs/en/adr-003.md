**Context.** The site is bilingual ES/EN and must rank in both languages.

**Decision.** `@nuxtjs/i18n` with the `prefix_except_default` strategy: Spanish at `/`, English at `/en`, with per-language `hreflang` and canonical. The no-prefix (single URL) option was discarded for SEO reasons.

**Consequences.** Each language is independently indexable and shareable; switching language is SPA navigation (instant), only the URL changes.
