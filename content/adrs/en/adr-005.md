**Context.** Long-form content (about, cases, ADRs) needs to be versionable, bilingual and easy to edit.

**Decision.** Prose and documents in Markdown with `@nuxt/content` (Zod-validated frontmatter, one file per language); structured records in `data/*.ts` and UI chrome in i18n.

**Consequences.** Editing a case or ADR means opening a `.md` — no touching components or escaping JSON; content lives in git, is diff-able and schema-validated.
